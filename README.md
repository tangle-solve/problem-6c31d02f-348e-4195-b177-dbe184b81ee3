### CHEM-000273: Enantioselective capture of unbound electrogenerated carbocations
- **Subfield:** Round 2
- **Open:** Trap directly electrogenerated, non-catalyst-bound tertiary carbocations with predictable enantioselectivity before rearrangement or solvent capture, without covalently attached chiral auxiliaries.
- **Status:** A 2024 method controls chiral α-imino carbocations using primary-amine catalysis. Freely diffusing tertiary carbocations present a distinct unresolved selectivity problem.
- **Type:** experimental
- **Why:** Enables asymmetric substitutions at crowded carbon centers from oxidizable feedstocks.
- **Key works:** Q. Lin et al. (2024), *Enantioselective SN1-type reaction via electrochemically generated chiral α-Imino carbocation intermediate*, [DOI:10.1038/s41467-024-50945-2](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-50945-2).
- **Related:** Biophysics: molecular recognition faster than diffusion-controlled background reactions.
